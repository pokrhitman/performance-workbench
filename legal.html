Performance Workbench is an independent, educational reference for investment performance measurement. It explains and lets you interactively verify how methods such as simple return, time-weighted return (TWR), money-weighted return (IRR/MWR), Modified Dietz and unit-based (NAV-per-unit) fund performance — including national conventions such such as Germany's BVI method — are calculated.

Nothing on this site constitutes investment, financial, tax or legal advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security, fund or other investment. The explanations, formulas and worked examples are provided for learning purposes only and are not a substitute for advice from a qualified, licensed professional who knows your specific circumstances.

No warranty of accuracy

The methods implemented in this site's formula engine are built and tested against publicly documented conventions and hand-worked examples, but they are provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, express or implied. Performance Workbench does not guarantee that any calculation, explanation or example on this site is complete, current or free of error. If you're using this site to sanity-check a real, disputed performance number, treat it as a second opinion to reason from — not as the final word in place of your own records, your provider's documentation, or professional advice.

Your data stays in your browser

This site has no server-side backend and no live market-data connections of any kind. Any portfolio data you type in or import as a CSV file into the verification widgets is processed entirely in your own browser, using plain JavaScript — it is never uploaded, transmitted or stored anywhere by this site. Closing or refreshing the page clears it.
